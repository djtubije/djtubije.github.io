Reflection on my College Life

Technical Milestones

During my time as a Software Engineer & Systems Analyst intern, my most significant contribution was the design and development of a script to automate webtool audits. Before this automation, the process was likely manual and prone to human error. By implementing this solution, I learned the importance of efficiency and reliability in enterprise software.

This task challenged my problem-solving skills, requiring me to understand the existing system architecture deeply before writing code. Seeing the script successfully run and save time for the team was a rewarding experience that solidified my passion for backend development and automation.


Professional Growth

Beyond the code, Globe Telecom provided me with an immersion into the corporate agile environment. Participating in daily stand-ups taught me how to communicate technical progress concisely and how to raise blockers effectively.

Furthermore, the code review sessions were invaluable. Receiving feedback from senior engineers helped me adopt best practices, write cleaner code, and understand that software engineering is a collaborative effort, not a solitary one. I learned to view constructive criticism as a tool for growth.


Conclusion

My internship from September to December 2025 was a pivotal step in my career. It bridged the gap between academic theory and real-world application. I am leaving Globe Telecom not just with better coding skills, but with a professional mindset ready to tackle complex challenges in the tech industry.
