Reflection on my College Life

My Benildean Journey

As a second-degree student, my college experience is unique compared to my peers. I focus primarily on efficiency and professional growth rather than just the social aspects of college life. I prioritize long-term rewards over short-term thrills. My goal is to become a successful Software Engineer, a career that perfectly aligns with my ambition to see the world and work from anywhere.

I am committed in improving my soft and technical skills continuously. I aim to become the best developer that I can be. Through this portfolio, it could serve as a bridge for me to hone my skills in programming that will serve as my tool for my career.

Learning is a continuous process

During my academic years at college, I learned various programming languages that proved invaluable during my On-The-Job Training. I also confirmed my belief that learning is a continuous process, it does not stop at the tertiary level but continues throughout one's career. Learning in the workplace differs from learning in a classroom setting, in fact I believe that I further developed my interpersonal skills during my On-The-Job Training Program at the company where I worked.
